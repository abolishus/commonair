Lower the creator share of subscriptions from 71% to 65%

TProposed by the treasury working group·Opened Oct 11·Closes Oct 21, 18:00 UTC

Summary

Move six points of every subscription to infrastructure for twelve months to fund a second origin region, then revert automatically.

Why

Peak egress is above what one region carries comfortably. Relays now serve about a third of traffic, but the origin is still a single point of failure.

What changes

  • Creator share: 71% → 65% from Nov 1, 2026 to Oct 31, 2027.
  • The six points go to a ring-fenced infrastructure account, reported monthly.
  • Tips are unaffected.
[i]

Creator council review · advised against, 3–2

“Supports the goal, not the mechanism. A temporary cut lands hardest on small channels. Prefer a tiered contribution.” Full review
