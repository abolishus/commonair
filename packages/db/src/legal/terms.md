## 5. Relays and peer-to-peer delivery

To keep costs low and the platform independent, video can be delivered with help from the
community:

- **Peer-to-peer (P2P) delivery.** While you watch, your browser may share pieces of the stream it
  has already received with other viewers of the same stream, over WebRTC. This uses some of your
  upload bandwidth. You can turn P2P sharing off in your settings at any time.
- **Relays.** Members may run relay servers that carry streams for other viewers. Relay operators
  agree to the relay rules shown when they register a relay, must report bandwidth honestly and
  must not inspect, record or alter the streams they carry. We may suspend relays that misreport
  or misbehave.

remove points (for example to correct an error or abuse), or turn points off; points end when the
channel turns them off, when your account ends, or when the service ends. Points are not a
deposit, a currency or property of yours.
