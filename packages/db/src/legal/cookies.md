This policy explains the cookies and browser storage {{instanceName}} uses at {{publicUrl}}.

## The short version

- **First party only.** Every cookie and stored value is set by {{instanceName}} itself. We do not
  use advertising, analytics, social-media or other third-party cookies, pixels, trackers or
  fingerprinting scripts, and the site loads no remote scripts or fonts.
- **Nothing for marketing.** Nothing we store is used to build a profile of you or shared with
  advertisers.
- **You choose the optional parts.** Essential storage is always on because the site cannot work
  without it. Everything else is off until you turn it on, and you can change your choice at any
  time from the cookie settings link in the site footer.

## Essential (always on)

These are needed for the service you asked for:

- **Session cookie.** Keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only and secure, and it ends when you sign
  out or after a period of inactivity.
- **Security cookies.** Protect sign-in and forms against cross-site request forgery and bots.
- **Consent record.** Remembers the choices you made on this page. If you are signed out, we store
  a random identifier in a first-party cookie so we can keep your choice; it is not linked to
  anything else about you.
- **Playback device id.** A random identifier in your browser's storage, used for verified viewer
  counts and abuse prevention (see the privacy policy). It is not a cross-site identifier.

## Preferences (optional)

If you allow preferences, we remember settings on your device so you do not have to set them
again: theme, player volume and quality, chat display options and similar. If you decline, the
site still works and uses defaults on each visit. Signed-in users' account settings are stored on
the server as part of their account, not in this category.

## Peer-to-peer delivery

Peer-to-peer video sharing is not a cookie, but it is a choice: you can switch it off in your
settings. When it is on, your browser connects to other viewers of the same stream through our own
tracker; see the terms of service for details.

## Your choices are recorded

When you save your choices, we record which categories you allowed, the version of this policy you
saw, and when. If this policy changes in a way that affects optional storage, we ask again.

## Clearing storage

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Doing so signs you out
and resets your preferences and your consent choices.
