{{instanceName}} respects the rights of creators, including the streamers who own this co-op. This
policy explains how to report content that infringes your copyright, and how to respond if your
content was removed by mistake. It follows the notice-and-takedown process of the United States
Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), and we apply the same process to notices from other
countries where the law allows.

## Sending a takedown notice

Send your notice to our designated agent at {{contacts.dmca}}. To be complete, it must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or someone authorised to act for
   them.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed (or a representative list if there
   are several).
3. The exact location of the material on {{instanceName}}: the address of the channel, VOD or clip,
   and for long videos the timestamps.
4. Your contact details: name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright
   owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that
   you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Before you send a notice, please consider whether the use could be fair use or covered by another
exception. Knowingly false notices can make you liable for damages.

## What we do with a notice

When we receive a complete notice we promptly remove or disable access to the identified material
and notify the uploader with a copy of the notice (we may remove your personal contact details
first, except where the law requires us to share them). For a live stream we may end the
broadcast. Incomplete notices get a reply explaining what is missing.

## Sending a counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, or that your
use is lawful, you can send a counter-notice to {{contacts.dmca}} or file it from the removal
notice in your account. It must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed
   or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction
   of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, any
   judicial district in which the co-op may be found), and that you will accept service of process
   from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

We forward a complete counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell
us within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action to stop the infringement, we
restore the material.

## Repeat infringers

We terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright. A notice counts towards this
only if it was not successfully countered or withdrawn. You can see notices against your account,
and appeal a strike, from your account settings.

## Other intellectual property complaints

For trademark or other rights complaints, or anything this policy does not cover, write to
{{contacts.general}}.
