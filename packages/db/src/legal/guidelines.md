{{instanceName}} belongs to the people who stream and watch on it. These guidelines describe how
we treat each other here. They apply to streams, VODs, clips, chat, profiles, bots and anything
else you publish.

## Be decent to people

- No harassment, threats, stalking or encouraging others to target someone, on or off stream.
- No hateful conduct: do not attack people for who they are, including race, ethnicity, national
  origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or serious
  illness.
- No sharing other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, documents, private
  photos) without their consent.
- Respect a "no". If someone asks you to stop contacting them, stop.

## Keep it safe

- Nothing that sexualises minors, ever. We report it to the authorities.
- No content that encourages self-harm or suicide. If you are struggling, please reach out to a
  local crisis line; if someone is in danger, contact emergency services.
- No credible threats, incitement to violence, or glorification of violent attacks.
- No dangerous stunts that put you or others at serious risk.
- No illegal activity, including selling illegal goods or services.

## Label mature content

Graphic violence, strong language, and sexual themes short of nudity may be streamed only on
channels or streams labeled **mature**, which only signed-in adults who confirmed they are 18+ can
see. Sexual activity and explicit nudity are not allowed. When in doubt, label it.

## Be honest

- Do not impersonate people, other channels or the co-op's staff.
- Do not manipulate the platform: no viewbots, purchased views or follows, follow-for-follow
  rings, or automated accounts made to inflate numbers, votes or payouts. These hurt every
  streamer who plays fair and every member who shares the co-op's income.
- Disclose sponsorships and paid promotion.
- No spam, scams, phishing or malware.

## Respect creative work

Only stream and upload what you have the right to use. Copyright holders can send a notice under
our copyright (DMCA) policy; repeat infringement leads to account termination.

## Bots and tools

Chat bots and applications must identify themselves as bots, use the official API with only the
scopes they need, and follow the same rules as people. The account that runs a bot is responsible
for what it does.

## Channel moderation

Streamers and their moderators may set stricter rules for their own channels, use word filters,
and time out or ban users from their chat. Channel moderators may not use their tools to harass
people or to enforce rules that break these guidelines.

## What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before: removal of the content, a
warning, a chat timeout, a temporary suspension of features or of the account, or a permanent ban.
We tell you what we did and why, unless doing so would put someone at risk or the law forbids it,
and you can appeal from your account. When possible, appeals are reviewed by someone other than
the person who made the original decision.

## Reporting

Use the report button on a stream, clip, message or profile, or write to {{contacts.abuse}}. For
conduct concerns about co-op staff or members in governance spaces, write to {{contacts.conduct}}.
Reports are confidential; we do not tell the reported person who reported them.
