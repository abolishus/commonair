When {{instanceName}} restricts your content or your account under our community guidelines or
because the content is illegal, we send you a **statement of reasons**. This notice explains what
a statement contains. The wording of each rule below is the explanation your statement quotes.
Statements made under an unreviewed draft of this notice say so.

## About this statement

A statement is a record of one decision. It is sent by email and is listed on your account's
appeals page ({{publicUrl}}/settings/appeals), which you can read even while your account is
suspended. It says:

- **what we restricted**: the content or account, the kind of restriction (removed, disabled,
  hidden behind the mature label, limited, suspended, or a warning with no restriction) and when
  it ends, if it ends. Restrictions apply everywhere {{instanceName}} is available;
- **the facts**: what led to the decision and whether it followed a notice from someone else or
  our own checks. We never name the person who sent a notice about intimate images;
- **automated means**: whether software detected the content and whether software or a person
  made the decision. When software decided, we tell you the threshold it used, not a score;
- **the rule or law we relied on**: the clause of our guidelines, or the law, quoted below;
- **how to challenge it**: see the last section.

We keep statements for as long as we keep the audit record of the decision itself.

## Clauses

<!-- clause: mature.unlabelled -->

### Mature content without the mature label

Graphic violence, strong language and sexual themes short of nudity are allowed only on channels
or streams labelled mature. Our checks found such content on a channel without the label, so we
added it. Only signed-in adults who confirmed their age can now see the channel. Nothing was
removed.

<!-- clause: safety.sexual-explicit -->

### Sexual activity or explicit nudity

Sexual activity and explicit nudity are not allowed anywhere on {{instanceName}}, including on
channels labelled mature.

<!-- clause: safety.graphic-violence -->

### Extreme graphic violence

Content that shows extreme graphic violence, or glorifies violent attacks, is not allowed, even
with the mature label.

<!-- clause: safety.weapons -->

### Dangerous weapons

Content that shows weapons in a way that threatens people or puts them at serious risk is not
allowed.

<!-- clause: safety.self-harm -->

### Self-harm and suicide

Content that encourages self-harm or suicide is not allowed. If you are struggling, please reach
out to a local crisis line; if someone is in danger, contact emergency services.

<!-- clause: safety.violence -->

### Threats and incitement

Credible threats, incitement to violence and glorification of violent attacks are not allowed.

<!-- clause: safety.minors -->

### Safety of minors

Nothing that sexualises or endangers minors is allowed, ever.

<!-- clause: safety.illegal-activity -->

### Illegal activity

Our guidelines do not allow illegal activity, including selling illegal goods or services. A
decision under this rule applies our guidelines; it is not a finding by a court.

<!-- clause: decency.harassment -->

### Harassment

Harassment, threats, stalking and encouraging others to target someone are not allowed.

<!-- clause: decency.hate -->

### Hateful conduct

Attacking people for who they are is not allowed.

<!-- clause: honesty.impersonation -->

### Impersonation

Pretending to be another person, another channel or our staff is not allowed.

<!-- clause: honesty.spam -->

### Spam and scams

Spam, scams, phishing and malware are not allowed.

<!-- clause: creative.copyright -->

### Other people's creative work

Only stream and upload what you have the right to use. Copyright notices follow our copyright
policy.

<!-- clause: guidelines.general -->

### Our community guidelines

A moderator found that the content or conduct broke our community guidelines. The statement
names the decision; the moderator's note, when there is one, is shown on your appeals page.

<!-- clause: illegal.ncii -->

### Intimate images shared without consent

Sharing intimate images of a person without their consent is not allowed, and the law in many
places requires us to remove them quickly once someone asks. We acted on a request from a person
depicted, or because the content matched images already removed for this reason. We do not tell
you who made the request.

## How to challenge this decision

<!-- section: redress -->

If you think we got it wrong, you can appeal from your account's appeals page
({{publicUrl}}/settings/appeals), also while your account is suspended. Someone other than the
person who made the decision reviews your appeal when possible. You can also write to
{{contacts.abuse}}. If you live in the European Union, you may be able to take the dispute to a
certified out-of-court dispute settlement body; the operator of {{instanceName}} names the body
it works with here once it has chosen one. You can always go to the courts.
