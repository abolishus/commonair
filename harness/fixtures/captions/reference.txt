Good evening, and welcome to the stream. Tonight we are going to build a small wooden boat in the garden. First, we cut the long boards and sand every edge until it feels smooth. Then we fit the pieces together and hold them in place with strong glue. While the glue dries, we can talk about the weather. It rained all morning, but now the sky is clear and the air is warm. Next, we paint the boat a bright shade of blue. When the paint is dry, we carry the boat down to the harbor and watch it float.
